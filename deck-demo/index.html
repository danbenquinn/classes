$$\Delta(m\dot y)=2m|\dot y|\ \text{per hit}$$
$$F_\text{avg}=\frac{2m|\dot y|}{2\ell/|\dot y|}=\frac{m\dot y^2}{\ell}$$
$$p=\frac{F_\text{avg}}{\ell^2}=\frac{n\,m}{\ell^3}\,\overline{\dot y^2}=\rho\,\overline{\dot y^2}$$
$$\Longrightarrow\quad\boxed{\ p=\tfrac13\,\rho\,\overline{|\dot{\underline r}|^2}\ }$$
