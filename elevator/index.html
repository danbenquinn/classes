Every elevator carries a plate stating how much it is rated to lift. It is written in pounds, which is a force — so it converts straight into newtons.

  1. Find the capacity plate inside the car. It is usually beside the door or above the button panel, and it gives a capacity in pounds.
  2. Convert that capacity to newtons. One pound-force is 4.448 N.

  3. Now round it to the nearest kilonewton.

    ⚠Round the answer, don't truncate it.

  4. The two-digit number you get is the passcode you seek.
